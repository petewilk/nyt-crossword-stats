Your crossword stats, one click away

See your NYT Mini and Daily solve times: medians, best times, how often you used hints, and how you've changed over the years.

Crossword stats

Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar

    Chrome: press ⌘ Shift B on a Mac, or Ctrl Shift B on Windows.

    Safari: choose View → Show Favorites Bar, or press ⌘ Shift B.

  2. Drag the button onto the bar

    Press and hold the black Crossword stats button above, drag it up to the bar, and let go. A new bookmark called “Crossword stats” appears.

  3. Open the NYT crossword page and click it

    Go to nytimes.com/crosswords and make sure you're logged in. Then click the Crossword stats bookmark.

    Your stats load in about 3–4 minutes. Keep the tab open while it works. The Mini appears first and the Daily follows.

  4. Save or share

    Save image saves a picture of your stats to send to friends. Download CSV saves the raw data as a spreadsheet file. Press Esc or × to close.

Is it safe?

The bookmark runs only in your own browser. It reads your solve history from the NYT the same way the crossword page does, and it never sends your data or login anywhere else. The code is open to read.

Something's not working?

It needs a computer. It won't work on a phone or tablet, or in the NYT Games app.

If clicking the bookmark in Safari does nothing, open Safari → Settings → Advanced, tick Show features for web developers, then choose Develop → Allow JavaScript from Smart Search Field.

If you see an error, click Copy error details and send the text to whoever shared this page with you.
